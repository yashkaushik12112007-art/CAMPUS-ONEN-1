CampusOne

Your Entire College in One Place

A unified, role-based campus operating portal connecting everyday academic information, administrative clearance workflows, and student services.

⚠️ The Core Problem

Campus Information is Broken & Fragmented

📢

Scattered Notice Boards

Critical circulars, exam dates, and scholarship notices are lost across WhatsApp groups, paper notice boards, and PDFs.

🚶‍♂️

Physical Office Queues

Students stand in queues just to check if their anti-ragging affidavit or hostel clearance was verified or rejected.

❓

Zero Next-Day Preparedness

Students scramble every morning finding which room has their lab, who is teaching, and whether assignments are due.

💡 The CampusOne Solution

Three Integrated Role-Based Workspaces

🏛️

1. Campus Home (Public)

Open portal for circulars, event registrations, canteen live availability, and professor cabin finder.

🎓

2. Student Portal (Personal)

Timetable, 75% attendance health monitor, internal marks, My Documents status tracker, and demo fee receipts.

🛡️

3. Faculty & Admin Portal

1-Click document verification/rejection, official notice broadcasting, and office hour availability management.

⭐ Our Flagship Differentiator

"Tomorrow at College"

Instead of making students dig through multiple pages, CampusOne automatically synthesizes the next academic day into one high-impact card:

📅 Next-Day Classes & Lab Locations

Exact time slots, course codes, professors, and room/lab numbers so students never walk to the wrong classroom.

⚠️ Immediate Action Items

Pending assignments due tomorrow, urgent document clearance deadlines, and relevant campus notices.

🛠️ Backend Architecture & Privacy Rules

Built with Scale, Privacy & Security

Full-Stack Foundation

HTML5, CSS3, ES6 Modular JavaScript, and native zero-dependency HTTP server. Firebase Authentication ready.

Cloud Firestore Collections

13 structured collections (users, students, documents, timetables, attendance, marks, notices, canteen, etc.).

Privacy-by-Design

Role-based access via firestore.rules. Lost & Found strictly masks personal phone numbers and student IDs.

🤖 Hackathon Innovation Highlight

CampusBot AI – Live Intelligence

Embedded directly across all portal pages, CampusBot AI allows students and faculty to query live university data using conversational natural language:

"What classes do I have tomorrow?"

Returns exact lectures, professors and lab rooms for the next morning.

"Check my attendance health"

Identifies subjects with <75% shortage and tells how many lectures to attend.

"Where is Dr. Sunita Rao?"

Pulls cabin Block B-304, published office hours, and consultation availability.

🚀 Production Roadmap

From Hackathon Prototype to Production

Phase 1: Official SIS/ERP Connector

Integration with university ERP databases via OAuth2 and approved student information protocols.

Phase 2: Real-Time Mobile Push

Native Android/iOS PWA with FCM push notifications for class cancellations and room shifts.

Phase 3: Digital Campus Wallet

Unified UPI/Smart-card integration for hostel mess, examination fees, and campus library deposits.
